SPDX identifier for ISS License

Here’s a clean, SPDX‑style identifier you can start using:

• Identifier: ISS-1.0
• Full name: ISS License 1.0
• Short description: International System Support / Scaffolding License, version 1.0


How to use it in your files

In source files:

SPDX-License-Identifier: ISS-1.0


In your ISS License 1.0.md header:

ISS License 1.0
SPDX-Identifier: ISS-1.0


In your README.md:

Licensed under the ISS License 1.0 (`ISS-1.0`).
See `ISS License 1.0.md` for details.
